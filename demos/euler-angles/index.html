Euler Angles: Precession, Nutation, and Spin

Here a gyroscope is used to illustrate the Euler angles in a 3-2-3 rotation. The outer ring is fixed in space. The angles are generally designated ψ, θ, φ or α, β, γ.

Browser port of a Wolfram Demonstration. Drag in the picture to rotate the view, scroll to zoom (Mathematica's "Rotate and Zoom in 3D"). All computation runs in your browser.
