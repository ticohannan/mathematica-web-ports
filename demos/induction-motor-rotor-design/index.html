AC Induction Motor Rotor Design

You can adjust the three principal parameters related to induction motor rotor design. Approximate values for three specific NEMA designs are provided by means of buttons.

Details

By adjusting the rotor resistance (RR), the rotor reactance (XR), and the blocked rotor voltage (EBR), you can observe the interactive effects of the parameters. The buttons provide a quick means of setting the parameters to the approximate NEMA design types of A, B, and D.

Browser port of a Wolfram Demonstration. All computation runs in your browser. Differences from the original: a value typed into a slider's field is kept within the slider's range; the "0" under the origin of the speed axis is not drawn; the arrows use the browser's fonts. The switch "errors of the original" can correct the "max torque" label when the rotor resistance exceeds the reactance (see below).
