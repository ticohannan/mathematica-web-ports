Line Parameterization

Every straight line in the plane can be written as x cos θ + y sin θ = ρ: ρ ≥ 0 is its distance from the origin and θ the direction of its normal. Set ρ with the slider and θ with the dial. (This caption is the port's: the lecture notebook has none.)

Details

The blue arrow is the normal from the origin to the line, of length ρ; the red arc is the angle θ from the x axis, from 0 to 2π; the small square marks the right angle at the foot point, where the line (purple) meets the normal. Lines through the origin (ρ = 0) have two parameters, θ and θ + π; every other line exactly one. This normal form is the parameterization of lines used, for example, by the Hough transform.

Port notes: the dial snaps to multiples of π/32 and shows angles from −π to π, while the picture measures θ from 0 to 2π, as in the original (see the design document, E-LP-01).
