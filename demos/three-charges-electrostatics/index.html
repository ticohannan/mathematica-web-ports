Electrostatics of a Linear System of Three Charged Particles

This Demonstration explores the electrostatics of a one-dimensional system of three charged particles, labeled qt, q1 and q2.

Use the sliders to vary the charges (in microcoulombs, μC) and positions (in meters, m) of the particles. Check "plot net force" to graph the net electric force (in newtons, N) on the the "target particle" qt due to the charges q1 and q2. Check "plot net field" to graph the net electric field (in meganewtons per coulomb, MN/C) created by q1 and q2 at the location xt of particle qt. Check the "show data at xt" box to display a table of the values of F1t (force on qt due to q1), F2t (force on qt due to q2), Fnet (net force on qt), E1 (field that q1 produces at xt), E2 (field that q2 produces at xt) and Enet (the net electric field at xt). Check "show equilibrium data" to show the equilibrium position xe of the target particle.

When only one finite equilibrium position is found, forces and fields are also reported in the equilibrium table. Use the upper-left figure to monitor the magnitude/direction of the individual forces on qt and the upper-right figure to monitor the magnitude/direction of the individual fields at xt (in both figures, the longest arrow corresponds to the force/field with the largest intensity, but the arrows are not to scale).

Browser port of a Wolfram Demonstration. All computation runs in your browser. The charge sliders run over the values −20 … 20 without 0, as the original's. Placing xt exactly on x1 or x2 (by typing the value) shows "Indeterminate" in the table and leaves out the arrows of the two small figures.
