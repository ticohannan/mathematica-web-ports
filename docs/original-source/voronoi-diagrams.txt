
### [Title]
Voronoi Diagrams

### [Section]
Caption<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
The Voronoi diagram for a set of points S in the plane is a partition of the plane into convex polygons, each of which consists of all the points in the plane closer to one particular point of S than to any other. You can also use \[AltKey]-click (\[CommandKey]-click on Mac) with your mouse to add or delete points.

### [Section]
Initialization Code<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [CodeText]
If you provide initialization code, include a SaveDefinitions->True option in the Manipulate.

Manipulate<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Input]
