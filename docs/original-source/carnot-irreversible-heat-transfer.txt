
### [Title]
Carnot Cycles with Irreversible Heat Transfer

### [Section]
Caption<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
This Demonstration models a Carnot cycle as either a heat engine or a heat pump. Change the temperature differences between the reservoirs and the Carnot cycle with sliders. The entropy changes for the reservoirs (ΔSubscript[S, h] and ΔSubscript[S, c]) and the overall entropy change ΔSubscript[S, total] are calculated. When the temperature differences between the reservoirs and the engine/pump are zero, the total entropy change is zero and the process is reversible. The entropy change of the engine/pump, which is at steady state, is zero. All energies and entropy changes are per unit time, since these are continuous processes, but the time scale is arbitrary. The cycle efficiency η is calculated for the heat engine, and the coefficient of performance COP is calculated for the heat pump. As the temperature differences between the reservoirs and the engine/pump increase, the efficiency/coefficient of performance decreases. For the heat engine, Subscript[Q, H] is held constant at 1250 J, and for the heat pump, Subscript[Q, C] is held constant at 600 J.

### [Input]
Manipulate[
Module[{Th,Tc,TH,TC,QH,QC,η,COP,W,ΔSh,ΔSc,cycle,labels},
Th=If[csb==1,500,325];Tc=275;

TH=Th+If[csb==1,-ΔT1,ΔT1];
TC=Tc+If[csb==1,ΔT2,-ΔT2];

QH=If[csb==1,-1250,-QC*(TH)/(TC)];
QC=If[csb==1,-QH*(TC)/(TH),-600];

η=(TH-TC)/(TH);COP=(TC)/(TH-TC);

W=If[csb==1,η*QH,-(QC)/(COP)];

ΔSh=QH/Th;ΔSc=QC/Tc;

cycle=Graphics[{
Circle[{0,0},2],
{Thick,Arrowheads[If[csb==1,Abs,Reverse]@{0,-0.03,-0.03,-0.03}],
Blue,Arrow[{{-Sqrt[3],1},{Sqrt[3],1}}],Arrow[{{Sqrt[3],-1},{-Sqrt[3],-1}}],
RGBColor[0,.6,0],Arrow[{{Sqrt[3],1},{Sqrt[3],-1}}],Arrow[{{-Sqrt[3],-1},{-Sqrt[3],1}}]},
Arrowheads[If[csb==1,{0,0.05},{-0.05,0}]],
Arrow[{{2,0},{5,0}}],(*work*)
Arrow[{{0,5},{0,2}}],(*to/from hot res*)
Arrow[{{0,-2},{0,-5}}],(*to/from cold res*)
Line[{{-5,5},{5,5}}],(*hot res*)
Line[{{-5,-5},{5,-5}}],(*cold res*)
{EdgeForm[Black],
If[csb==1,LightRed,LightGreen],Rectangle[{5.10,-0.5},{8.95,0.5}],
White,Rectangle[{-5.65,-0.5},{-2.45,0.5}],
LightBlue,Rectangle[{-6.25,-2.5},{-1.75,-1.5}],
LightYellow,Rectangle[{-6.25,3.5},{-1.75,4.5}],Rectangle[{-6.25,-3.5},{-1.75,-4.5}],Rectangle[{1.65,1.75},{6.35,2.75}],Rectangle[{1.65,-1.75},{6.35,-2.75}]}
}];

labels=Graphics[{
Text[Style["\<hot reservoir\>",18],{-5,5},{-1,-1}],Text[Style["\<cold reservoir\>",18],{-5,-5},{-1,1}],
Text[Style[Row@{Subscript[Style["\<W\>",Italic],"\<S\>"],"\< = \>",NumberForm[N@W,{4,0}],"\< J\>"},18],{7,0}],
Text[Style[If[csb==1,Row[{Style["\<η\>",Italic],"\< = \>",NumberForm[N@η,2]}],Row[{Style["\<COP\>",Italic],"\< = \>",NumberForm[N@COP,2]}]],18],{-4,0}],
Text[Style[Row@{Subscript[Style["\<T\>",Italic],#1],"\< = \>",#2,"\< K\>"},18],#3,#4]&@@@{{"\<h\>",Th,{0,5},{0,-1}},{"\<c\>",Tc,{0,-5},{0,1}},{"\<H\>",TH,{3.75,1},{0,0}},{"\<C\>",TC,{3.75,-1},{0,0}}},
Text[Style[Row@{Subscript[Style["\<Q\>",Italic],#1],"\< = \>",NumberForm[N@#2,{4,0}],"\< J\>"},18],#3,#4]&@@@{{"\<h\>",QH,{5,5},{1,-1}},{"\<c\>",QC,{5,-5},{1,1}},{"\<H\>",-QH,{4,2.25},{0,0}},{"\<C\>",-QC,{4,-2.25},{0,0}}},
Text[Style[Row@{"\<Δ\>",Subscript[Style["\<S\>",Italic],#1],"\< = \>",NumberForm[N@#2,{3,2}],"\< J/K\>"},18],{-4,#3}]&@@@{{"\<h\>",ΔSh,4},{"\<c\>",ΔSc,-4},{"\<total\>",ΔSh+ΔSc,-2}}
}];

Show[cycle,labels,ImageSize->{600,430}]
],
Grid[{{
Control[{{csb,1,"\<\>"},{1->"\< heat engine \>",2->"\< heat pump \>"},Setter}],Spacer@10,
Control[{{ΔT1,10,Row[{"\<|\>",Subscript[Style["\<T\>",Italic],"\<h\>"],"\< - \>",Subscript[Style["\<T\>",Italic],"\<H\>"],"\<\[VerticalSeparator]\>"}]},0,50,2,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Small}],
Control[{{ΔT2,12,Row[{"\<|\>",Subscript[Style["\<T\>",Italic],"\<c\>"],"\< - \>",Subscript[Style["\<T\>",Italic],"\<C\>"],"\<\[VerticalSeparator]\>"}]},0,50,2,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Small}]
}}]
]

Details & Citations<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
ΔSubscript[T, 1]=|Subscript[T, h]-Subscript[T, H]|,
ΔSubscript[T, 2]=|Subscript[T, c]-Subscript[T, C]|,

### [Text]
ΔSubscript[S, h]=(Subscript[Q, h])/(Subscript[T, h]),

### [Text]
ΔSubscript[S, c]=(Subscript[Q, c])/(Subscript[T, c]),

### [Text]
ΔSubscript[S, h]+ΔSubscript[S, c]=ΔSubscript[S, total],

### [Text]
where ΔSubscript[T, 1] and ΔSubscript[T, 2] are user-defined differences in reservoir and Carnot cycle temperatures (K). The subscripts h and c refer to the hot or cold reservoirs, and the subscripts H and C refer to the hot or cold temperatures of the Carnot cycle. T is temperature (K), ΔS is the change in entropy (J/K), and Q is heat gained or lost (J).

### [Text]
For a Carnot heat engine, heat is transferred from a hot reservoir to a cold reservoir and the engine does work (W). For a real process, Subscript[T, h]>=Subscript[T, H] and Subscript[T, c]<=Subscript[T, C]. The efficiency η of a Carnot engine, in terms of the engine temperatures, is:

### [Text]
η=(Subscript[T, H]-Subscript[T, C])/(Subscript[T, H]),

### [Text]
Subscript[Q, C]=-Subscript[Q, H] (Subscript[T, C])/(Subscript[T, H]),

### [Text]
\[VerticalSeparator]W\[VerticalSeparator]=η Subscript[Q, H].

### [Text]
For a Carnot heat pump, heat is transferred from the cold reservoir to the hot reservoir and work is added. For a real process, Subscript[T, h]<=Subscript[T, H] and Subscript[T, c]>=Subscript[T, C] to have a reasonable rate of heat transfer. The coefficient of performance COP is

### [Text]
COP=(Subscript[T, C])/(Subscript[T, H]-Subscript[T, C]),

### [Text]
Subscript[Q, H]=-Subscript[Q, C] (Subscript[T, H])/(Subscript[T, C]),

### [Text]
\[VerticalSeparator]W\[VerticalSeparator]=(Subscript[Q, C])/(COP).
