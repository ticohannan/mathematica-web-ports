### [Title]
Chart for a Torus

### [Section]
Caption<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
A torus (more precisely, a 2-torus) has the shape of the outer surface of a donut. A torus can be parametrized by two angular variables (Theta,Phi), each in[0,2π). A dissected torus can be pictured as equivalent to a rectangle in the two-dimensional Euclidean plane \[DoubleStruckCapitalR]^2, with opposite pairs of edges glued together. Locally, the 2-torus resembles \[DoubleStruckCapitalR]^2, but there is no global continuous mapping since the Euclidean plane extends to infinity. This Demonstration shows possible mappings from a 2-torus to \[DoubleStruckCapitalR]^2. The operation necessarily introduces discontinuities, where small changes of Theta or Phi create large jumps in the planar configuration. Two rotary dials determine a point (Theta,Phi) (highlighted in green) in the course of a transformation.

### [Text]
The 2-torus (or T^2) is a manifold that can be embedded in \[DoubleStruckCapitalR]^3, as illustrated by the 3D plot. A manifold is a topological space that locally resembles n-dimensional Euclidean space \[DoubleStruckCapitalR]^n in a neighborhood of each point. Locally, the neighborhood of any point on T^2 resembles one in \[DoubleStruckCapitalR]^2 (the Euclidean plane).

### [Text]
Globally, a manifold can differ from a Euclidean space; for example, the surface of the sphere S^2 is not topologically equivalent to a Euclidean space. Likewise the surface of a torus. As shown in this Demonstration, the torus can be split along its longest circumference to form a cylinder, and then this cylinder can be opened up into a rectangle. Both operations introduce discontinuities where small changes of Theta or Phi create large jumps in the configuration.

### [Text]
In the context of manifolds, these mappings are called charts. To avoid discontinuities, you can switch from one chart to another, much like a pilot can switch from a map of North America to a map of the Pacific when flying from New York to Tokyo. A collection of overlapping charts is called an atlas.
