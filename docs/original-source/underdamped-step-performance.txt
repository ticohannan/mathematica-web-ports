
### [Title]
\<Performance Characteristics for Step Response of an Underdamped Process\>

### [Section]
Caption<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
Consider an underdamped second-order process with a transfer function (the ratio of output to input of a system), given by G(s)=(Subscript[K, g])/(τ^2 s^2+2 ζ τ s+1), where Subscript[K, g] is the steady-state gain, τ is the time constant, and ζ is the damping coefficient (with 0<ζ<1). The process is subjected to a step input U(t)=(M)/(s). This Demonstration shows plots of the response y(t)=\[ScriptCapitalL]^(-1)(G(s) U(s)) and finds its performance characteristics: overshoot a/b, decay ratio c/a, time to rise Subscript[t, r], time to first peak Subscript[t, p], and period of oscillations P. You can vary the values of M, Subscript[K, g], τ, and ζ. Here, the values of ζ are restricted to the interval[0.05,0.3] in order to clearly identify a, b, c, and P in the plot of the output function y(t).

Manipulate[Module[{Omega,yanal,sol1,sol2,sol3,p1,Overshoot,DecayRatio,P,tp,InvTau},InvTau=1/τ;Omega=Sqrt[InvTau^2-(InvTau ζ)^2];
yanal[t_]:= Kg M+(-2 Kg M InvTau  ζ/Omega+ Kg M InvTau ζ/Omega) Exp[-ζ InvTau t] Sin[Omega t]-Kg M  Exp[-ζ InvTau t] Cos[Omega t];
tp=Pi τ/Sqrt[1-ζ^2];
sol1=FindRoot[yanal[t]== M Kg,{t,0,tp}]//Chop;sol2=FindMaximum[yanal[t],{t,tp,tp+0.2}];
P=2 Pi/Omega;
sol3=Quiet@FindMaximum[yanal[t],{t,tp+P}];
Overshoot=Exp[-Pi ζ/Sqrt[1-ζ^2]];
DecayRatio=Exp[-2 Pi ζ/Sqrt[1-ζ^2]];
p1=Plot[yanal[t],{t,0,3 P},Frame->True,GridLines->Automatic,AspectRatio->1.5,FrameLabel->{Style["\<time\>",16],Style["\<output\>",16]},PlotRange->All,PlotStyle->{Red,Thick},ImageSize->400{1,1}];

### [Text]
The overshoot is equal to a/b=exp(-π ζ/Sqrt[1-ζ^2]).

### [Text]
The decay ratio is equal to c/a=(a/b)^2=exp(-2 π ζ/Sqrt[1-ζ^2]).

### [Text]
The period of oscillation is P=2 π τ/Sqrt[1-ζ^2].

### [Text]
The time to first peak is given by Subscript[t, p]=π τ/Sqrt[1-ζ^2].

### [Text]
The rise time is the solution Subscript[t, r] of y(Subscript[t, r])=M Subscript[K, g] and 0<Subscript[t, r]<Subscript[t, p].
