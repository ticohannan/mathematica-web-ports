
### [Title]
Solar Energy Yield for Three Mounting Systems

### [Section]
Caption<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
\<This Demonstration compares daily solar energy yield obtained with five type of mounting systems: fixed (yellow), horizontal (orange dashed), polar (blue dashed), vertical axis (red dashed), and two-axis tracker (green), based on solar data and latitude of the SunMine project in Kimberley, BC, Canada.\>

### [Section]
Initialization Code<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Input]
sunvect[dd_,hr_,lat_]:=Module[{ζ=Cos[0.017453* ((10. +dd)*360./365.24 +360*0.0167/π* Sin[0.017453 (-2. +dd)* 360./365.24])]},{-\[Sqrt](1.0\[VeryThinSpace]-0.158236* ζ^2) Sin[(1)/(12) (12.-hr) π],0.39779 * Cos[° lat]* ζ+Cos[(1)/(12) (12-hr) π] \[Sqrt](1.0-0.158236*ζ ^2) Sin[° lat],Cos[° lat] Cos[(1)/(12) (12.-hr) π] \[Sqrt](1.\[VeryThinSpace]-0.158236* ζ^2)-0.39779 * ζ* Sin[° lat]}]

### [Input]
efull[dd_,hr_,lat_]:=
With[{sr=sunvect[dd,hr,lat],\[Iota]0x=1380.,Alpha1=0.87,
Beta1=0.17,εx=(1+0.033412*Cos[2*π*(dd-2)/365 ])},
{sr,{\[Iota]0x*εx*Alpha1*Exp[-Beta1/sr[[3]]],\[Iota]0x*εx*sr[[3]]*(0.271-0.2939*Alpha1*Exp[-Beta1/sr[[3]]])}}]

### [Input]
pnlrot[{σ_,ρ_}]:={-Sin[° ρ] Sin[° σ],Cos[° ρ] Sin[° σ],Cos[° σ]}

### [Input]
polrot[σ_,hr_]:=RotationMatrix[(hr-12)*15 °,RotationMatrix[-σ °,{1,0,0}].{0,1,0}].RotationMatrix[-σ °,{1,0,0}].{0,0,1}

### [Input]
trkall[dd_,hr_,lat_,σ_,ρ_]:=With[{ef=efull[dd,hr,lat]},Block[{asp,prt,x},prt=Insert[pnlrot[#]&/@{{σ,ArcTan[ef[[1,1]],ef[[1,2]]]/ ° - 90},{(π/2-ArcSin[ef[[1,3]]])/ °,0},{σ,0}},polrot[σ,hr],2];asp=#.ef[[1]]&/@prt;
Flatten@{Total @ef[[2]],ef[[2,1]]*#*Boole[#>0]&/@asp+ef[[2,2]]}]]

### [Text]
This graphic is provided in the context of the SunMine project in Kimberley. It compares the solar energy captured by five different solar panels' mounting systems:

### [Text]
\<\[Bullet] fixed at at an angle normally set to optimize the yearly energy yield,\>

### [Text]
\[Bullet] horizontal (rotating around an east-west axis),

### [Text]
\<\[Bullet] polar (rotating around an axis more or less parallel to the Earth's axis),\>

### [Text]
\<\[Bullet] vertical axis tracker ( a fixed-slope panel rotating from east to west to follow the sun's azimuth),\>

### [Text]
\<\[Bullet] dual axis ( following the sun in both directions (azimuth and elevation).\>

### [Text]
\<The graphic shows the total daily solar energy yield for the five systems, based on a clear sunny day, every day. Solar energy is composed of a varying proportion of direct and diffuse irradiation. The efficiencies of the trackers are greater on days with clear skies than on cloudy days.\>
