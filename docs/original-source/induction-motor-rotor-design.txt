
### [Title]
AC Induction Motor Rotor Design

### [Section]
Caption<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
\<You can adjust the three principal parameters related to induction motor rotor design. Approximate values for three specific NEMA designs are provided by means of buttons.\>

Details & Citations<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
By adjusting the rotor resistance (Subscript[R, R]), the rotor reactance (Subscript[X, R]), and the blocked rotor voltage (Subscript[E, BR]), you can observe the interactive effects of the parameters. The buttons provide a quick means of setting the parameters to the approximate NEMA design types of A, B, and D.
