
### [Title]
\<Simulating Vehicle Suspension with a Simplified Quarter-Car Model\>

### [Section]
Caption<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
\<The simplified quarter-car suspension model is basically a mass-spring-damper system with the car serving as the mass, the suspension coil as the spring, and the shock absorber as the damper.\>

### [Text]
\<This Demonstration lets you explore the affect of different suspension parameters and road conditions on the vertical motion of the car.\>

### [Text]
The mathematics of the system are based on the differential equation of the spring-damper suspension: c t x'-c t y'+k t x-k t y+m t x''\[LongEqual]0, which, after a Laplace transform, results in the transfer function G (s)=(c s+k)/(c s+k+m s^2).\[LineSeparator]The Mathematica 8 functions TransferFunctionModel and OutputResponse were used to calculate the car movement with no need to solve the differential equation.

Manipulate[
Module[{model,output,top,bottom,ζ,Omegap},
model=TransferFunctionModel[(k+c s)/(k+c s+m s^2),s];
output=Re[OutputResponse[model,h Sin[2π fb(*bump frequency*) t],t]];
top=First[1+output]/.t->time;bottom=-1+h Sin[2π fb time];
ζ=NumberForm[c/2/Sqrt[m k],{3,2}];Omegap=(*resonant frequency*)NumberForm[Re[Sqrt[1-c^2/(2 k m)]Sqrt[k/m]],{3,2}];
Column[{
Plot[Evaluate[{h Sin[2π fb t],output}],{t,time,time+5},PlotRange->{-.2,.2},Background->LightGray,PlotStyle->{Blue,Directive[Thick,Red]},Axes->{True,False},Ticks->None,AspectRatio->.2,ImageSize->400],
Graphics[{
Black,Arrow[{{0,top},{-1.25,top}}],
Thickness[.01],Line[{{0,bottom},{0,bottom+1}}],Rectangle[{-0.15,bottom+.8},{.15,bottom+1+c/5}],
Blue,Line[{{0,top},{0,top-.5}}],Line[{{-0.2,top-.50},{.2,top-.50}}],Line[{{-0.2,top-.5},{-.2,top-1.5}}],Line[{{0.2,top-.5},{.2,top-1.5}}],
Black,Line[{{0,bottom},{0,bottom-.48}}],Thin,Arrow[{{0,bottom},{-1.25,bottom}}],spring[top,bottom, .25k],
Thickness[.02],Line[{{-.1,bottom},{.1,bottom}}],Circle[{0,bottom-.5},.15],Thickness[.01],
Rectangle[{-.75,top},{.75,top+m}],
Inset[Column[{Style["\<damping ratio ζ\>"==ζ,14],Style["\<resonant frequency\>"==Omegap,14]}],{1.75,0}],
Inset[Style[Row[{Style["\<y\>",Italic],"\<(\>",Style["\<t\>",Italic],"\<)\>"}],14,Blue],{-1.75,-1}],Inset[Style[Row[{Style["\<x\>",Italic],"\<(\>",Style["\<t\>",Italic],"\<)\>"}],Red,14],{-1.75,1}],Plot[-1.74+h Sin[2t+2π fb time],{t,-3,3},Axes->False,PlotStyle->Darker[Green],PlotRange->{Automatic,{-2.5,2.5}},Filling->-2.5,FillingStyle->Brown][[1]]},
PlotRange->{{-3,3},{-2.5,2}},Axes->{False,True},Ticks->{None,Join[{#,ToString[#-1]}&/@Range[.75,1.25,.25],{#,ToString[#+1]}&/@Range[-1.25,-.75,.25]]},AxesOrigin->{-1.25,0},ImageSize->475]}]],
Style["\<car suspension
animation\>",Bold],
"\<start / pause / reset\>",{{time,0,"\<\>"},0,20,.25,Trigger,AnimationRate->1(*DisplayAllSteps->True*)},
Row[{"\<elapsed time in sec = \>",Dynamic[NumberForm[time,{3,1}]]}],
Delimiter,
Style["\<suspension parameters\>",Bold],
"\<\>",
"\<damping coefficient: c\>",
{{c,.85,"\<\>"},0,1,.001,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
"\<\>",

"\<\>",

{{m,1,"\<\>"},0.25,1,.001,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},Delimiter,
Style["\<road condition\>",Bold],
"\<\>",
"\<road bump height\>",
{{h,.1,"\<\>"},0,.2,.01,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
"\<\>",
"\<bump frequency: fb\>",
{{fb,1,"\<\>"},0.1,2,.01,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
TrackedSymbols->True,
