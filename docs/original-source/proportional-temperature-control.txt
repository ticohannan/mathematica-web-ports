
### [Title]
Proportional Temperature Control

### [Section]
Caption<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
\<In this Demonstration, water is flowing through the tank and is being heated to attain a target temperature. A proportional control sensor provides feedback to the heater. The tank's outlet water temperature (red) often oscillates before converging. The sensor experiences a delay in measuring the temperature (blue), demonstrated by the time lag in the blue curve.\>

Initialization Code<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [CodeText]

### [Section]
Manipulate<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Input]
Manipulate[Module[{rho,cp,Kp,TauSens,TauQ,QC,Tsens,sol1,TR,Q},rho=1;cp=4.19;Kp=300;TauSens=2;TauQ=1;
QC[t_]=Max[0,Q0+Kp (TRset-Tsens[t])];
sol1=NDSolve[{V rho cp D[TR[t],{t,1}]== F rho cp (T0-TR[t])+Q[t],
      D[Q[t],{t,1}]== (QC[t]-Q[t])/TauQ, 
      D[Tsens[t],{t,1}]== (TR[t]-Tsens[t])/TauSens,TR[0]== T0,
      Tsens[0]== T0,Q[0]== 2500},{Q[t],Tsens[t],TR[t]},{t,0,150}];
Plot[{Evaluate[Tsens[t]]/.sol1,Evaluate[TR[t]]/.sol1},{t,0,150},PlotRange->All,ImageSize->{500,350},ImagePadding->{{50,45},{35,50}},Frame->{True,True,False,False},FrameLabel->{"\<time (hours)\>","\<temperature C\>"},PlotLabel->Style[Column[{Row[{"\<volume = \>"<>ToString[V]<>"\< \!\(\*SuperscriptBox[\(m\), \(3\)]\)\>","\<water flow = \>"<>ToString[F]<>"\< \!\(\*SuperscriptBox[\(m\), \(3\)]\)/h\>","\<heat input = \>"<>ToString[Q0]<>"\< kJ/h\>"},Style["\<    |    \>",GrayLevel[.5]]],Row[{"\<outlet temperature = \>"<>ToString[TRset]<>"\< C\>","\<initial tank temperature = \>"<>ToString[T0]<>"\< C\>"},Style["\<    |    \>",GrayLevel[.5]]]},Center],SingleLetterItalics->False],AxesOrigin->{0,0}]],{{V,100,"\<tank volume\>"},10,100,1},{{F,10,"\<water flow\>"},1,20,1},{{Q0,2500,"\<heat input\>"},1000,3000,1},{{TRset,80,"\<outlet temperature\>"},20,150,1},{{T0,20,"\<initial tank temperature\>"},20,100,1},TrackedSymbols:>{V,F,Q0,TRset,T0},ContinuousAction->False]
