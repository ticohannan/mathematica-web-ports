
### [Title]
Quarter-Car Suspension Model with Double Spring

### [Section]
Caption<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Text]
\<The quarter-car suspension model simulates a double-spring mass system. The mass attached to the spring represents the body of the car while the free mass represents the wheel, tire, and suspension.\>

### [Text]
\<The effect of an input from a single road hump or from a continuous sinusoidal input are simulated with graphical and animated representation of the system response. You can control the masses, stiffness, and damping for each spring and the input data.\>

### [Section]
Manipulate<<PaneSelectorBox>>

### [Input]
Manipulate[
Module[{eqn1,eqn2,ivals,dispx,f,followu,follows,bottoms,topu,lens,as,bs,cs,bottomu,ground,lenu,au,bu,cu,dispgraphics},

eqn1=m1 x1^\[Prime]\[Prime][t]+k1 (x1[t]-x2[t])+c1 (x1^\[Prime][t]-x2^\[Prime][t])==0;
eqn2=m2 x2^\[Prime]\[Prime][t]+k1 (x2[t]-x1[t])+c1 (x2^\[Prime][t]-x1^\[Prime][t])+k2 x2[t]+c2 x2^\[Prime][t]==f[t];
ivals={x1[0]==0,x1^\[Prime][0]==0,x2[0]==0,x2^\[Prime][0]==0};

f[t]=CalcForce[t,type,k2,wid,h,Omega ];

soln=NDSolve[{{eqn1, eqn2}, ivals},{x1[t],x2[t]},{t,0,maxtime}];
sols[t_]=x1[t]/.soln[[1,1]];
solu[t_]=x2[t]/.soln[[1,2]];

ζs=NumberForm[N[c1/(2Sqrt[m1 k1])],{3,2}];
fs=NumberForm[N[Re[Sqrt[1-c1^2/(2 k1 m1)]Sqrt[k1/m1]]/(2 Pi)],{3,2}];
Omegas=NumberForm[N[Re[Sqrt[1-c1^2/(2 k1 m1)]Sqrt[k1/m1]]],{3,2}];
ζu=NumberForm[N[c2/(2Sqrt[m2 k2])],{3,2}];
fu=NumberForm[N[Re[Sqrt[1-c2^2/(2 k2 m2)]Sqrt[k2/m2]]/(2 Pi)],{3,2}];
Omegau=NumberForm[N[Re[Sqrt[1-c2^2/(2 k2 m2)]Sqrt[k2/m2]]],{3,2}];

dispx = Plot[{sols[t],solu[t]},{t,0,maxtime},PlotRange->{-0.25,0.25},
Background->LightGray,PlotStyle->{Darker[Blue],Darker[Red]},Axes->{False,False},Ticks->None,AspectRatio->.3,ImageSize->400];
followu=Graphics[{PointSize[Large],RGBColor[.49,0,0],Point[{run,solu[run]}]}];
follows=Graphics[{PointSize[Large],RGBColor[0,0,0.49],Point[{run,sols[run]}]}];
startys = sols[run];
startyu = solu[run];
bottoms=0.5 + startys;
topu=-0.5+startyu;
lens =bottoms-topu-0.2;
as={{startx,bottoms},{startx,bottoms-0.1}};
bs=Table[ {startx+dia*Sin[π*n-π/2],0.5+startys-0.1-n * lens/11},{n,10}];
cs={{startx,topu+0.1},{startx,topu}};

bottomu=-1.25 + startyu;
ground = CalcGround[type,run,wid,h,Omega];
lenu =bottomu -ground-0.2;
au={{startx,bottomu},{startx,bottomu- 0.1}};
bu=Table[ {startx+dia*Sin[π*n-π/2],bottomu-0.1-n * lenu/11},{n,10}];
cu={{startx,ground+0.1},{startx,ground}};

dispgraphics = Graphics[{ 
Line[Join[as, bs,cs]],
Line[Join[au,bu,cu]],
Darker[Blue],
Rectangle[{startx-1,0.5 + startys},{startx+1,1.5 + startys}],
Darker[Red],
Rectangle[{startx-0.5,-1.25 + startyu},{startx+0.5,-0.5 + startyu}],
Black,
Inset[Style[
Column[{
Row[{Subscript["\<ζ\>", 1],"\< = \>",ζs}],
Row[{Subscript["\<Omega\>", 1],"\< = \>",Omegas,"\< rad/s\>"}],
 Row[{Subscript["\<f\>", 1],"\< = \>", fs,"\< Hz\>"}]}],14],
{1.75,0.5}],
Inset[Style[
Column[{
Row[{Subscript["\<ζ\>", 2],"\< = \>",ζu}],
Row[{Subscript["\<Omega\>", 2],"\< = \>",Omegau, "\< Hz\>"}], Row[{Subscript["\<f\>", 2],"\< = \>", fu,"\< Hz\>"}]
}],14],{1.75,-1.25}],
Plot[CalcCurve[t,type, run,wid,h,Omega],{t,-3,3},Axes->False,PlotStyle->Darker[Green],PlotRange->{Automatic,{-2.5,2.5}},Filling->-2.5,FillingStyle->Darker[Green, .6]][[1]],
PointSize[Large],Pink,Point[{startx,ground}]},
PlotRange->{{-3,3},{-2.5,2}},Axes->{False,False},AxesOrigin->{-1.25,0},ImageSize->400];

Column[{
Show[dispx,followu,follows],Show[dispgraphics]
}]],

{{run,0,"\<play\>"},0,maxtime,.05,Trigger,AnimationRate->1, AppearanceElements->{"\<PlayPauseButton\>","\<ResetButton\>"}},
{{run,0,"\<time\>"},0,maxtime,.05,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
Delimiter,
Style["\<mass\>",Bold],
{{m1,400,Subscript[Style["\<m\>",Italic], 1]},200,1000,1,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
{{m2,30,Subscript[Style["\<m\>",Italic], 2]},5,100,1,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
"\<\>",
Style["\<stiffness\>",Bold],
{{k1,20000,Subscript[Style["\<k\>",Italic], 1]},10000,200000,1000,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
{{k2,80000,Subscript[Style["\<k\>",Italic], 2]},10000,200000,1000,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
"\<\>",
Style["\<damping\>",Bold],
{{c1,2000,Subscript[Style[c,Italic], 1]},0,10000,100,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
{{c2,0,Subscript[Style["\<c\>",Italic], 2]},0,10000,100,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
Delimiter,
Style["\<input\>",Bold],
{{type,1,"\<type\>"},{1->"\<single bump\>",2->"\<sinusoidal\>"},RadioButton,Appearance->"\<Vertical\>"},
{{h,0.15,"\<height\>"},0,0.2,.01,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
{{wid, 1.0,"\<duration\>"},0.1,5,.1,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
{{Omega, 1.0,"\<frequency\>"},0,3,.1,Appearance->"\<Labeled\>",ImageSize->Tiny},
"\<\>",
SynchronousUpdating->True,
ControlPlacement->Left,
SaveDefinitions->True,
TrackedSymbols->All,
Initialization:> 
(maxtime=10;
startx = -0.75;
dia = 0.2;
CalcPulse[ti_,tmin_,length_, he_]:=If[ti>=tmin&&ti<=(tmin+length),he Sin[(π/length)*(ti-tmin)],0];
CalcForce[ti_,typ_,k_,wi_,he_,omega_ ]:=If[typ== 1,k CalcPulse[ti, 2, wi, he],k he Sin[2 π omega ti]];
CalcCurve[ti_,typ_, ru_,wi_,he_,omega_]:=If[typ==1,-2+CalcPulse[ti+ru-startx, 2, wi, he],-2+he Sin[2π omega (ru+ti - startx)]];
CalcGround[typ_, ru_,wi_,he_,omega_]:=If[typ==1,-2+CalcPulse[ru, 2, wi, he],-2+he Sin[2π omega ru]]
)]

\<The Demonstration solves the two second-order differential equations that describe a double-spring system:\>

### [Text]
Subscript[m, 1]Subscript[x, 1]''+Subscript[k, 1](Subscript[x, 1]-Subscript[x, 2])+Subscript[c, 1](Subscript[x, 1]'-Subscript[x, 2]')=0,

### [Text]
Subscript[m, 2]Subscript[x, 2]''+Subscript[k, 1](Subscript[x, 2]-Subscript[x, 1])+Subscript[c, 1](Subscript[x, 2]'-Subscript[x, 1]')+Subscript[k, 2]Subscript[x, 2]+Subscript[c, 2]Subscript[x, 2]'=f.

### [Text]
The forcing function f is modeled as either a single cosine shaped bump, such as a road speed hump, or a continuous sinusoidal input. A numerical solution is employed to show the evolution of the system.
