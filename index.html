Wolfram Demonstrations — Browser Ports

Re-implementations of three Mathematica Manipulate demonstrations as static web pages. Everything is computed and drawn in your browser; the server only delivers files.

Motion Planning for Robot Path around Obstacles

Polygonal robot, four polygonal obstacles, Minkowski-sum configuration space, visibility graph and A* shortest path.

2DSVGdrag locators

Three Parametrizations of Rotations

Euler ZYZ angles, axis/angle and roll-pitch-yaw describing the same rotation of a teapot.

3DWebGL

Euler Angles: Precession, Nutation, and Spin

A gyroscope with nested gimbals illustrating a 3-2-3 Euler rotation.
